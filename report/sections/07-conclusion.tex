\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a DBpedia-style knowledge base for Vietnamese football, provinces and
universities, covering the five tasks of the assignment. An OWL ontology of
18 classes and 54 properties is aligned to the DBpedia Ontology. 990 entities
were selected through Wikidata and their Vietnamese articles collected through
the MediaWiki API. Abstracts, infoboxes, categories and redirects were
transformed into 88{,}898 validated triples with stable IRIs, which OWL~2~RL
reasoning extends to 131{,}233. 777 entities are linked to English DBpedia, of
which 733 resolve to an article today, and all 990 to Wikidata. A server
dereferences the IRIs with content negotiation and offers resource pages, a
SPARQL editor and natural-language question answering. Reasoning is what
makes the data interoperable: it adds the DBpedia types and properties that
DBpedia clients expect, and the shortcut edges that turn multi-hop questions
into simple queries.

Future work follows from the limitations: a standard SPARQL Protocol endpoint
with query time limits; more classes, such as coaches and competitions,
together with page links; a link check against DBpedia at every build; and a
benchmark of Vietnamese questions to evaluate the question-answering tab.
